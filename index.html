The perfect parking recipe

  1. Follow the route to the mint-green parking space.
  2. Avoid parked cars, barriers, and the striped boundaries.
  3. Move the whole car inside the marked space.
  4. Release the controls to stop and straighten the car.
  5. Press Space / the joystick button, or stay still for one second.
Start with 100 points. Park for +50. Obstacles cost 20 and boundaries cost 10. You can always try again.

This is an arcade simulator: left and right move sideways. Level 3 asks you to reverse into the bay. Finish before the level's countdown runs out.

Keyboard controls are ready. Connect an ESP32 whenever you like.

Ready, plug, play.

  1. Upload the included joystick sketch to your ESP32.
  2. Connect the board with a USB data cable.
  3. Close the Arduino Serial Monitor so the port is free.
  4. Click Connect ESP32, then select your board.
  5. Move the joystick and watch the live values change.
Use a desktop browser with Web Serial support, such as Chrome or Edge. Permission is only requested when you click Connect.

Your game works without a board. If serial isn't available, keep playing with your keyboard or the on-screen buttons.
